## Top 3 value gaps (quantified, prioritized)

1. **Owner-dependence (Hub & Spoke).** Current score 3.
   Close to 4 over 12 months via a formal 90-day stand-aside test for the #2.
   → Estimated **+0.5 turn** ≈ **+$2.5M EV** on $5.0M Adjusted EBITDA.

2. **Customer concentration.** Top-1 at 22%; goal ≤18%.
   Diversify or backstop with multi-year contract + named-account growth in
   adjacent verticals.
   → Estimated **+0.25 turn** ≈ **+$1.25M EV**.

3. **Books quality.** Move to reviewed financials, then audit; pair with a
   sell-side QoE early so add-back support is built once.
   → Estimated **+0.25 turn** ≈ **+$1.25M EV**.

**Total estimated value gap: ~$5M EV (+1 turn).** Pre-tax payoff vs. an ~$1.5M
all-in incremental cost (audit uplift + QoE + advisory time) is highly accretive.

## Cadence
- **Re-score quarterly** (EPI "Dynamic Revaluation" pattern) and log progress in
  `memory/scp/deal-log.md`.
- Revisit composite vs. the 80-target before triggering an outreach phase.
- Owner's **personal & financial readiness** (the "three legs of the stool")
  reviewed at each quarterly check — independent of the business score.
