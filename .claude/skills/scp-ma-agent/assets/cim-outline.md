# SCP CIM Outline — Section-by-Section Template

The structured outline the `scp-ma-agent` skill drafts from `memory/scp/profile.md`
and `memory/scp/add-backs.md`. **Target length:** 60–100 pp (middle-market) or
30–60 pp (lower-MM). **Senior banker drafts and reviews — not first-year analysts.**

**Confidentiality discipline (apply throughout):**
- No customer names without explicit owner consent — disclose concentration as **% only**.
- No proprietary process details before deeper, post-NDA diligence.
- No non-public supplier or pricing data without redaction.

---

## Cover / Confidentiality
Project codename (e.g., **"Project Ozark"**), date, advisor logo, confidentiality
legend, distribution control (numbered copies in some processes).

## 1. Executive Summary (2–4 pp)
- One-paragraph elevator: what SCP makes, for whom, why interesting now.
- Headline financials: revenue, **adjusted EBITDA**, margin vs. ~16.6% foundry benchmark.
- 5–7 investment-highlight bullets (mirror §2).
- Transaction overview: advisor, structure preference, timing.

## 2. Investment Highlights (3–5 pp)
Foundry-specific multiple levers — **quantify every claim**:
- Diversified end markets with aerospace/defense exposure (premium lever).
- Low customer concentration (top-1 _x%_, top-5 _y%_).
- Value-add content (raw → machined / finished / assembly).
- Modern capex profile / clean environmental posture.
- Credible #2 / management depth (owner-independence).
- Tariff backdrop favoring domestic casting.

## 3. Company History & Business Model (2–4 pp)
Founding, ownership history, milestones, geographic footprint, current model.

## 4. Products & Services (4–8 pp)
- Casting processes (sand / investment / die / permanent mold).
- Alloy mix (gray iron / ductile / steel / aluminum / brass-bronze).
- Part-size envelope and volume profile (high-mix/low-vol vs. high-volume).
- Value-add content (machining / finishing / assembly / inspection).
- Certifications (AS9100 / Nadcap / ISO / IATF).
- Proprietary tooling / patterns / IP.

## 5. Operations (6–12 pp) — **foundry-critical section**
- Facility footprint (sq ft, owned vs. leased — flag related-party real estate).
- Furnaces (induction / cupola) — types and ages.
- Molding lines (DISA / Match, automated, manual) — ages and condition.
- Coremaking, finishing, machining cells.
- Capacity, throughput, utilization, lead times.
- Capex history (3–5 yr) and planned investment.
- Energy & scrap procurement / hedging.
- Quality (PPM) and safety (OSHA record).
- Environmental posture (permits current, no NOVs, sand reuse, RCRA/NESHAP).
- Workforce (headcount, union status, apprenticeship pipeline, retention).

## 6. Management Team (2–4 pp)
Org chart, bios for top 5–8 leaders, depth chart, succession plan, owner's
post-close role.

## 7. Customers & End Markets (4–6 pp)
- End-market mix chart (aero/defense, auto, ag, construction, oil & gas, etc.).
- Top customers as % of revenue — **NO NAMES** without consent.
- Tenure, contract structure, share-shift risk, pricing power, pass-through.
- Backlog / booked orders / pipeline.

## 8. Market & Competitive Landscape (3–5 pp)
- NAICS 3315 sizing, US global position, AFS outlook (refresh live).
- Tariff backdrop on imported steel/aluminum.
- Competitive set positioning by capability and end market.

## 9. Financial Performance (6–10 pp) — **anchor of the document**
- 3 yr + TTM P&L with bridge to **Adjusted EBITDA** (the bridge is the centerpiece — pull from `memory/scp/add-backs.md`).
- Revenue by product / customer mix / end-market mix.
- Margin trends; gross-profit bridges.
- Working capital, capex, free cash flow.
- Balance-sheet highlights.
- Projections (3–5 yr) with bottom-up build and key assumptions.

## 10. Growth Opportunities (3–5 pp)
Quantified and defensible only:
- Aerospace certification expansion / new program wins.
- Machining insourcing / vertical integration.
- New molding line or melt facility.
- Geographic / end-market adjacency.
- M&A roll-up in adjacent foundries.

## 11. Transaction Considerations (1–2 pp)
- Structure preferences (asset / stock / F-reorg / §338(h)(10) — see `references/tax-structuring.md`).
- Rollover-equity appetite, owner post-close role, retention plans.
- Process timeline.

## 12. Appendix
- Glossary, certifications detail, equipment list, environmental docs index,
  detailed customer/supplier roster (**post-NDA addendum, not in base CIM**),
  DCF / valuation backup.

## Draft-and-review workflow
1. Pull confirmed data from `memory/scp/profile.md` + `memory/scp/add-backs.md`.
2. Draft sections; mark every unconfirmed figure `(assumption — confirm)`.
3. Senior banker review before any external distribution.
4. Owner sign-off on §7 (customers) language and any name-disclosure.
5. Log each draft cycle and reviewer feedback in `memory/scp/deal-log.md`.
