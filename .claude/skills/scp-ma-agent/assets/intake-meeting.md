# SCP Intake Meeting — Question Script

Run this like a banker's first management meeting, **not** a form. Ask 1–3
related questions at a time, reflect back what you hear, save to
`memory/scp/profile.md` after each topic, and follow threads that open risk or
upside. It's fine to spread this across several sessions — track what's still
open in `memory/scp/deal-log.md`.

Open the meeting by (a) requesting the documents below, and (b) explaining you'll
walk through ~7 areas and the most important one is the owner's own goals.

---

## Documents to request (ask for uploads first)

Tell the user they can drop files into the repo or paste figures; you'll read them.

- **Financials** — 3 years + TTM P&L and balance sheet (and tax returns if no
  audit/review). Monthly trial balances if available.
- **Add-back support** — owner comp, family on payroll, related-party rent,
  one-time/non-recurring items, ERC, personal expenses run through the business.
- **Customer data** — revenue by customer (top 10–20), by end market, contract
  vs. PO, tenure; backlog/booked orders.
- **Cap table / ownership** — entity type (S-corp? C-corp? LLC?), owners, %s,
  any options/phantom equity, related-party real estate ownership.
- **Operations** — facility list, equipment/furnace & molding-line list with
  ages, capacity & utilization, capex history (last 3–5 yrs) and deferred capex.
- **Environmental** — air permits, any EPA/state NOVs, RCRA/foundry-sand
  handling, Phase I/II if any, NESHAP status.
- **People** — org chart, key-person roster (melt supervisor, master
  patternmaker), union status, headcount, turnover.
- **Other** — existing valuation/QoE/exit work, prior inbound buyer interest,
  any current advisor or NDA in place.

---

## Topic 1 — The business & casting capabilities

- Walk me through what SCP makes and for whom. What's the elevator pitch?
- **Casting process(es):** sand, investment (lost-wax), die, permanent mold?
- **Alloy mix:** gray iron, ductile iron, steel, aluminum, brass/bronze, other?
  (This drives the comp segment and which buyers to call — confirm it explicitly.)
- Part-size envelope and volumes (high-mix/low-vol vs. high-volume)?
- **Value-add content:** raw castings only, or machining / finishing / assembly?
  (Machined-and-finished trades meaningfully higher than pure castings.)
- Any proprietary patterns, tooling ownership, certifications (AS9100, ISO,
  Nadcap, IATF)?

## Topic 2 — Financials & the EBITDA story

- Revenue last 3 years + TTM; gross margin; reported EBITDA and margin.
- Run the **add-back schedule** with me (see `references/valuation.md` for the
  standard categories): owner comp vs. market replacement, family on payroll,
  related-party rent vs. market, one-time legal/consulting, ERC, COVID
  distortions, run-rate for new/lost contracts, growth vs. maintenance capex.
- Working capital: typical level, seasonality, any unusual AR/inventory.
- Debt, leases, related-party balances, off-balance-sheet items.
- Audited / reviewed / tax-basis only?

## Topic 3 — Customers & end markets

- Top 5 customers as % of revenue (top-1 and top-5 concentration).
- End-market mix: automotive, commercial vehicle, ag, construction, oil & gas,
  **aerospace/defense** (premium), industrial machinery, infrastructure/waterworks.
- Contract structure, tenure, share-shift / re-source risk, pricing power, pass-
  through of scrap/energy costs.
- Pipeline / new business / recent wins & losses.

## Topic 4 — Operations & capex

- Facilities, square footage, owned vs. leased (and who owns the real estate).
- Furnaces (induction/cupola), molding lines (e.g., DISA/Match), coremaking —
  ages and condition. Any modernization done or needed?
- Utilization, throughput constraints, lead times.
- Energy & scrap procurement — contracts/hedging for nat gas, electricity, scrap.
- Deferred capex (a value killer) vs. recent investment (a value story).

## Topic 5 — Environmental & regulatory

- Air permits current? Any open NOVs from EPA/state?
- Foundry-sand handling / beneficial reuse; RCRA status; NESHAP (iron & steel).
- Any known contamination, Phase I/II history, pending remediation?
- OSHA record. (Environmental is a foundry-specific diligence flashpoint.)

## Topic 6 — People & owner-dependence

- Org chart and depth below the owner. **Is there a credible #2 who could run it?**
  (Hub-&-Spoke / owner-dependence is one of the biggest value drivers.)
- Key-person risk: who holds the melt/metallurgy and patternmaking know-how?
- Headcount, union vs. non-union, apprenticeship pipeline, turnover/retention.
- What breaks if the owner steps away for 90 days?

## Topic 7 — The owner: personal & financial goals (most important)

Frame the "three legs of the stool": business value, **personal goals**,
**financial readiness**. Owners who skip this most often regret the sale.

- **Why sell, and why now?** Full exit, partial (rollover equity), recap,
  succession to family/management, or just getting ready/optionality?
- **Timeline:** sell now, or prepare over 1–5 years? (Best prep starts 2–5 yrs out.)
- **Number:** how much do you need from this, after tax, to be financially set?
  Have you done that math with an advisor?
- **Post-close:** do you want to stay on, for how long? Any roles for family?
- **Non-negotiables:** employees, name/legacy, community, buyer type (strategic
  vs. PE), location/jobs staying in Bentonville?
- **Personal readiness:** what will you do the day after? (The "75% regret"
  problem is usually about this, not price.)

---

## Closing the meeting

- Summarize what you heard and the 3–5 biggest value levers and risks you spotted.
- Confirm everything is saved to `memory/scp/profile.md`.
- Propose next steps (e.g., "I'll run a baseline valuation and a draft buyer
  Tier A; you get me the customer-by-revenue file and the furnace/molding ages").
- Log the meeting, decisions, and open items in `memory/scp/deal-log.md`.
