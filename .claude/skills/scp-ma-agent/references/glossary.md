# M&A Glossary — Owner-Facing

Short definitions for the jargon used throughout this agent. The agent should
spell out an acronym the first time it uses it in a session.

## Money & financials
- **EBITDA** — Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation, Amortization. The cash-earnings proxy buyers value off of.
- **Adjusted EBITDA** — Reported EBITDA plus *add-backs* (one-time / non-business / above-market items removed). The number on which the multiple is applied.
- **Add-back** — A line item removed from reported EBITDA to show "normalized" earnings (owner comp above market, family on payroll, one-time legal, etc.). Each must survive a QoE to be paid for.
- **TTM** — Trailing Twelve Months — the most recent 12 months of financials.
- **EV / Enterprise Value** — Equity value + debt − cash. The headline deal price.
- **EV/EBITDA multiple** — EV ÷ Adjusted EBITDA. Foundry segment ~5.3–6.2x; broad middle market ~9–10x.
- **Working capital** — Current assets − current liabilities; the operating cushion the buyer expects to inherit.
- **Capex** — Capital expenditures. *Growth* capex builds new capacity; *maintenance* capex sustains it.

## Documents & process
- **Teaser** — 1–2 page anonymized "blind" summary sent before an NDA. (See `assets/teaser-template.md`.)
- **NDA** — Non-Disclosure Agreement. Mutual, 18–24 mo confidentiality, sometimes employee non-solicit.
- **CIM** — Confidential Information Memorandum. The 30–100 page "selling book." (See `assets/cim-outline.md`.)
- **Mgmt presentation** — 1–3 hr meeting where buyers meet the management team after CIM review.
- **VDR / Data room** — Virtual Data Room hosting financials, contracts, and diligence docs (Datasite / Intralinks / Firmex).
- **QoE** — Quality of Earnings. Sell-side QoE done by CPA firms before going to market; buyer's QoE done during diligence.

## Offers & contracts
- **IOI** — Indication of Interest. Non-binding, ~30–45 days after CIM. Gives a valuation range, structure, financing plan.
- **LOI** — Letter of Intent. Binding only on confidentiality, exclusivity (30–90 days), expense reimbursement. Substantive terms non-binding.
- **Exclusivity** — Window in which the seller can talk only to that buyer; the price for accepting an LOI.
- **Definitive agreement (SPA / APA)** — The binding purchase agreement: stock purchase (SPA) or asset purchase (APA).
- **Reps & warranties** — Seller's written statements about the business in the SPA/APA; buyer relies on them.
- **Indemnification** — Seller pays back the buyer if reps prove wrong. Capped at a % of EV.
- **Escrow / holdback** — Cash held back from the seller for indemnity claims (now often 0.5–2% of EV when R&W is in place).
- **R&W insurance** — Representations & Warranties insurance. Replaces most of the escrow. ~2.5–3.0% rate-on-line in 2025; retention ~0.5% of EV.
- **MAC clause** — Material Adverse Change. Lets the buyer walk if the business deteriorates materially before close.
- **Working-capital peg** — Target WC at close; deviations true-up cash 60–90 days post-close.
- **Earnout** — Deferred payment contingent on post-close performance. Taxed when received.
- **Rollover equity** — Seller retains an equity stake in the post-deal company (typ. 10–25% in PE deals); often a "second bite."
- **Seller note** — Deferred payment as a note from buyer to seller; bears interest.

## Structure & tax
- **Asset sale** — Buyer buys the assets; buyer gets step-up in basis; seller pays ordinary tax on depreciation recapture.
- **Stock sale** — Buyer buys the shares; seller gets capital-gains treatment; buyer gets no step-up.
- **§338(h)(10) election** — Legally a stock sale, taxed as an asset sale. Needs corporate buyer + 80%+ stock + S-corp / consolidated C-sub target.
- **F-reorganization** — Holdco structure that lets an S-corp be acquired "asset-style" by any buyer (incl. PE LPs) with tax-deferred rollover. ~70% of PE S-corp acquisitions use it.
- **§1202 / QSBS** — Qualified Small Business Stock. Up to $10M or 10× basis of gain excluded on C-corp stock held 5+ years (assets <$50M when issued).
- **HSR** — Hart-Scott-Rodino antitrust notification. 2025 threshold ~$119.5M (refresh).

## Buyers
- **Strategic** — An operating company in the same / adjacent industry. Often pays the highest price due to synergies.
- **Financial / PE** — Private-equity sponsor. Holds 4–7 years, then resells. Typically wants rollover equity.
- **Family office** — Direct private capital with longer hold horizons. Often less aggressive process behavior.
- **Platform / Add-on** — In PE, a *platform* is the first deal in a roll-up; *add-ons* fold into it. Add-ons often pay closer to platform multiples than to standalone multiples.

## Foundry-specific
- **NAICS 3315** — North American Industry Classification System code for foundries.
- **Foundry sand** — Spent sand from molding; handled per RCRA; "beneficial reuse" programs are a value story.
- **NESHAP** — National Emission Standards for Hazardous Air Pollutants — EPA category that hits iron & steel foundries.
- **Phase I / Phase II ESA** — Environmental Site Assessment. Phase I = paper review; Phase II = sampling.
- **NOV** — Notice of Violation (EPA / state). Open NOVs depress the multiple.
- **DISA / Match line** — Common automated molding-line technologies; recent investment is a value marker.
- **AS9100 / Nadcap** — Aerospace quality certifications. Closing these is a multiple lever.
- **AFS** — American Foundry Society. Publishes the Metalcasting Forecast.
