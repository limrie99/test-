# Sell-Side Process Playbook — 7 Phases

Use to build SCP's timeline and task list, and to tell the owner what to prepare
when. Total active process from engagement to close is typically **6–10 months**;
exit *preparation* (readiness work) should start **2–5 years** earlier.

---

### Phase 0 — Pre-engagement / advisor "beauty contest" (2–6 wks)
3–5 advisors pitch with a **"valuation football"** (DCF / comps / public comps /
LBO floor), credentials, sector deal sheet, **proposed buyer universe** (often the
deciding factor), timeline, and fee proposal. Deliverable for SCP: shortlist +
selection rationale. See `references/advisors-and-fees.md`.

### Phase 1 — Engagement letter (1–2 wks)
Key clauses to negotiate: fee triggers (cash, assumed debt, earnouts, rollover,
seller notes); retainer + credit mechanics; **tail period 12–24 mo** with
named-buyer carve-outs; exclusivity; expense reimbursement; indemnification;
advisor information rights.

### Phase 2 — Preparation (8–14 wks) — *where the fee is earned*
- **Sell-side QoE** (4–6 wks): 3yr + TTM, proof of cash, normalized EBITDA,
  working-capital normalization, customer/revenue concentration, balance-sheet
  quality, GAAP adjustments. Cost: see `references/transaction-costs.md`.
- **Audit/review uplift** if only tax-basis books exist (~$25–75k, one cycle).
- **EBITDA normalization / add-back schedule** (see `references/valuation.md`).
- **CIM**: 30–60 pp (lower-MM) / 60–100 pp (MM). Sections: exec summary,
  investment highlights, history & model, products, **operations (processes,
  locations, workforce — critical for foundries)**, management, customers &
  revenue (concentration by % only, no names), market & competition, financials
  (historical + projections + **adjusted EBITDA bridge**), growth opportunities,
  structure, appendix. **Teaser** = 1–2 pp anonymized, sent pre-NDA.
- **Buyer universe** tiered A/B/C, annotated (see `references/buyer-universe.md`).

### Phase 3 — Outreach & process management (4–10 wks)
Phased outreach (Tier A first), monitor response rate. **Mutual NDA** (18–24 mo
confidentiality, sometimes employee non-solicit). CIM distribution to NDA signers.
**Management presentations** (1–3 hrs) for advancers.

### Phase 4 — IOI → LOI → Definitive (8–16 wks)
- **IOI** (non-binding): valuation range, structure, financing plan, conditions,
  diligence needs, timing. Due ~30–45 days after CIM.
- **LOI**: binding only on confidentiality, **exclusivity (30–90 days)**, expense
  reimbursement; substantive terms non-binding. **Evaluate on price AND certainty
  of close**, financing contingency, regulatory risk, cultural fit, retention,
  owner's post-close role, rollover/earnout (PE rollover often **10–25%**). See
  `references/offers` logic below.
- **Definitive (SPA/APA)**: reps & warranties, indemnification, escrow/holdback,
  R&W insurance, working-capital peg & true-up, MAC clause, regulatory approvals
  (**HSR threshold ~$119.5M in 2025 — refresh**).

### Phase 5 — Buyer diligence (6–12 wks)
**VDR** (Datasite / Intralinks / Firmex / SecureDocs). Q&A workflow, expert calls
(commercial, financial, tax, legal, **environmental — foundry flashpoint**: Phase
I/II, air permits, NESHAP, foundry-sand disposal, RCRA, OSHA). Advisor keeps
momentum and **prevents retrades**.

### Phase 6 — Negotiation & signing (2–6 wks)
- **Working-capital peg**: 12–24 mo seasonality-aware look-back, collar ±2–5% of
  peg, true-up 60–90 days post-close, independent-accountant dispute resolution.
- **R&W insurance**: ~**2.5–3.0% rate-on-line** (firming — refresh), retention
  ~0.5% of EV stepping to ~0.4% after 12 mo. With R&W, escrow/holdback often
  **0.5–2% of EV** (vs. 10%+ historically).
- **Indemnity caps**: general reps 15–25% of EV; fundamental reps up to 100%;
  tax/title up to 100% with longer survival.

### Phase 7 — Closing & post-close (2–8 wks; transition 6–24 mo)
Funds flow, escrow funded, R&W bound, **TSA** (3–12 mo for shared services/IT/
finance), earnout administration, working-capital true-up (60–90 days), advisor
success fee and legal fees paid.

## Evaluating competing offers (apply in Phase 4)

Score each IOI/LOI on a structured rubric, not just headline price:
- **Headline EV** and structure (cash / rollover / earnout / seller note).
- **Net-to-seller after tax** (depends on structure — see `tax-structuring.md`).
- **Certainty of close** — financing (committed vs. "highly confident"),
  diligence remaining, regulatory risk, track record of closing.
- **Speed / exclusivity ask.**
- **Non-price fit** — employees/jobs, owner's post-close role, name/legacy,
  buyer type (strategic vs. PE), cultural fit.
- **Earnout/rollover quality** — achievability, control, downside.
Build a comparison table and a recommended counter for each. Save offers and the
recommendation to `memory/scp/deal-log.md`.
