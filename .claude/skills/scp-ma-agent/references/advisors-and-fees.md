# Advisors & Fees Reference — Foundry / SCP

Two jobs: (1) recommend the right **advisor tier/firm** for SCP's size, and
(2) **model fees** so the owner knows the all-in cost. SCP at ~$5M EBITDA / ~$30M
EV sits in the **lower-middle-market boutique → middle-market specialist** band.

### What separates great from mediocre advisors
Senior banker on the deal (not just the pitch); CIM written by seniors, not
first-years; **curated not catalog** outreach; ability to run a credible auction
with competing bids; discipline in LOI→close to prevent retrades; sector
knowledge that finds add-backs and shapes the narrative.

## Fee structures (model these for SCP)

Per FIRMEX survey: ~40% use a Lehman scale, ~35% flat %, ~18% accelerated; ~56%
credit the retainer against the success fee.

| Schedule | Formula | Use case |
|---|---|---|
| Classic Lehman | 5% / 4% / 3% / 2% / 1% on each successive $1M | Mostly obsolete sell-side |
| **Modified Lehman (3-3-2-1-1)** | 3% first $1M, 3% next, 2%, 1%, 1% on balance | Workhorse above ~$20M EV |
| **Double Lehman (10-8-6-4-2)** | 10% first $1M, 8%, 6%, 4%, 2% on balance | Common sub-$10M EV / harder deals |
| Double-% / Modern | held at 3% above $8M | Lower-MM specialists |
| Compressed (2.5-2-2-1-1) | — | Large-cap / capital markets |

**Retainer:** $10–25k/mo (lower-MM), $25–75k/mo (MM); often credited.
**Tail:** 12–24 mo after termination for buyers introduced during the engagement.

### Worked fee examples
- **$5M EV, Double Lehman** = $500k (10% blended).
- **$20M EV:** Classic ~1.5%; Modified 3-3-2-1-1 ~1.5–2%; Double 10-8-6-4-2 ~4%.
- **$30M EV, Modified Lehman** ≈ ~$700k–$1.0M with retainer (~2.5–3.5%); minimum
  success fees of $400–500k are common at this size.
- **$50M EV, Modified Lehman** ≈ ~$1.4M (~2.8%).
- **$100M EV, Modified** ≈ ~$2.4M (~2.4%); **$250M** ≈ 1.2–1.5%.

> Compute the actual blended fee tier-by-tier from the agreed EV and schedule;
> show the owner the marginal rate on the last dollar (it falls as EV rises),
> and remember fee triggers may include assumed debt, earnouts, and rollover.

Save the chosen advisor, schedule, retainer, and tail to `memory/scp/profile.md`.
