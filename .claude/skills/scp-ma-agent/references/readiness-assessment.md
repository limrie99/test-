# Sale-Readiness Assessment — Foundry / SCP

Output: a **score**, a **value gap** (current vs. potential multiple), and
**90-day-sprint action items** for the weakest drivers. This is the wedge —
the work that should start **2–5 years before** a targeted exit and produces the
multiple expansion that pays for everything else.

Frame it with the **"three legs of the stool"**: business value, personal goals,
financial readiness. Owners who ignore the last two are in the ~75% who later
regret the exit — assess all three.

---

## A. Value Builder System — 8 drivers (score each 1–5)

1. **Financial performance** — quality/consistency of earnings; reviewed/audited?
2. **Growth potential** — credible, fundable growth runway.
3. **Switzerland Structure** — independence from any one customer, supplier, or
   employee.
4. **Valuation Teeter-Totter** — cash generation vs. cash consumption (working
   capital, capex intensity — foundries are heavy here).
5. **Recurring revenue** — contracted / repeat vs. one-off project work.
6. **Monopoly Control** — pricing power, differentiation, switching costs.
7. **Customer Satisfaction** — retention, NPS-equivalent, repeat order rate.
8. **Hub & Spoke** — can it run without the owner? (Owner-dependence.)

Benchmark: typical businesses score ~**59/100**; raising the score toward **80**
has been associated with acquisition offers ~**71% above average**. Convert the
1–5 driver scores to a /100 composite and report against these anchors.

## B. EPI 4 C's — capital inventory

- **Human capital** — management depth, key-person coverage, apprenticeship pipeline.
- **Customer capital** — concentration, contract strength, end-market diversity.
- **Structural capital** — documented SOPs, systems, IP/patterns/tooling, data.
- **Social capital** — culture, reputation, supplier & community relationships.

## C. Foundry / industrial overlay (the SCP-specific risks)

Score these explicitly — they're where foundry deals get repriced:

- **Customer concentration** — top-1 > 25% or top-5 > 50% is a red flag.
- **Single-vertical exposure** — e.g., automotive-heavy without aero/defense balance.
- **Environmental posture** — air permits current, no open NOVs, foundry-sand
  reuse program, RCRA/NESHAP documented, no contamination.
- **Capex / asset condition** — furnace & molding-line ages; deferred capex is a
  value killer; recent modernization is a value story.
- **Key-person risk** — melt/metallurgy supervisor, master patternmaker.
- **Real estate** — owner-owned and leased to the business at non-market rates?
- **Books quality** — GAAP vs. tax-basis; audited/reviewed vs. compiled.
- **Owner-dependence** — credible #2 who can run operations.
- **IT/cyber & ESG documentation** — increasingly diligenced.

---

## Scoring → value gap → actions

1. **Score** A (8 drivers), B (4 C's), and C (overlay). Weight the overlay
   heavily for SCP — it's what moves the foundry multiple.
2. **Translate to multiple impact** using the levers in `references/valuation.md`
   (e.g., fixing top-1 concentration from 40%→25% and adding a credible #2 can be
   worth ~+0.75–1.25 turns combined).
3. **Value gap** = (potential multiple − current multiple) × adjusted EBITDA.
   State it in dollars; it's the motivator.
4. **90-day sprints** — for each weak area, a concrete, time-boxed action:
   - Reduce customer concentration / diversify into aero or defense.
   - Document SOPs; build the operations #2 who can run without the owner.
   - Proactively clean environmental records; renew/confirm air permits.
   - Reset the capex narrative (modernize one molding line or melt facility).
   - Move to reviewed/audited financials; formalize the add-back support.
   - Address related-party rent / family-comp normalization.
5. **Re-score periodically** (EPI "Dynamic Revaluation" idea) and log progress in
   `memory/scp/deal-log.md`.

Output format: a short scorecard table (driver → score → 1-line rationale), the
composite /100 vs. 59/80 anchors, the dollar value gap, and a prioritized action
list with owners and 90-day targets. Save the scorecard to `memory/scp/profile.md`.
