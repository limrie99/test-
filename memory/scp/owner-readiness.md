## 6. Regret triggers — addressed proactively
The most common "I regret the sale" causes:
- **Money was wrong** — number didn't actually meet retirement need. _Mitigation: §2 wealth-advisor math complete and conservative._
- **Bored / lost purpose** — no plan for day after. _Mitigation: §3 written and pressure-tested._
- **People got hurt** — buyer changed culture / cut jobs / closed plant. _Mitigation: §5 non-negotiables documented in LOI and reps._
- **Family rift** — successor / family compensation unclear. _Mitigation: §4 family conversations done before LOI._
