## Scoring logic
- **Certainty (1–5):** financing strength, diligence remaining, regulatory risk, buyer's recent close record, exclusivity ask. A 4–5 offer at slightly lower EV often beats a 1–2 offer at higher EV.
- **Non-price fit (1–5):** owner's non-negotiables — employees/jobs, legacy/name, location/Bentonville, buyer-type preference. Score honestly; this is what the owner regrets later.
- **After-tax net** is the only price number that matters. Translate each headline EV through the chosen structure (see `references/tax-structuring.md`) — F-reorg vs. §338(h)(10) vs. straight stock vs. asset materially changes the rank order.
